# WBS Destination Page Structure — Paris Reference

## Layout
- Full-bleed hero image with city name + tagline overlay
- Breadcrumb: Country · Region
- Stats bar: [N] Curated hotels | Region | [N] Journal guides
- **Two-column layout below:**
  - LEFT (main): Editorial prose + Editor's Intelligence + Best Time to Visit + How to Choose + Hotel listings
  - RIGHT (sidebar): "Destination Intelligence — [City] by the Numbers"

## Intelligence Sidebar — Data Points
1. **Annual Visitors** — 17.4M international (2024) — Source: Euromonitor
2. **Michelin Stars** — 127 restaurants in Paris (2026 Guide) — Source: Wikipedia/Michelin
3. **5-Star Hotels** — 116 (7th most globally) — Source: CN Traveller ME (2025)
4. **UNESCO Sites** — 54 in France, 4th globally
5. **Avg Hotel Rate** — $174 all hotels avg (2025) — Source: all hotels avg
6. **Peak Season** — Apr–Jun · Sep (fashion weeks, mild weather)

## Sources Section (bottom of sidebar)
- Euromonitor Top 100 City Destinations 2024
- Wikipedia — Michelin-starred restaurants in Paris (2026 Guide)
- CN Traveller ME — Cities with most 5-star hotels (2025)

## Editor's Intelligence (dark panel)
- 3–4 diamond bullet points with insider knowledge
- Practical, specific, actionable advice
- Not generic tourism copy

## Best Time to Visit
- 12-month calendar with colour coding: Peak / Good / Avoid
- One paragraph explanation

## How to Choose
- 4 numbered scenarios (e.g., "For the full Palace experience...", "For contemporary luxury...")

## Hotel Listings
- Grid of hotel cards with rating badges (FORBES ✦✦✦✦✦, W50B #19 etc.)
- Save to wishlist + Save to Reading List buttons
- "See all N hotels in [City]" CTA

## From the Journal
- 3 article cards with category + read time + title

## More in [Region]
- 6 related destination cards

## SAparts Equivalent — City Hub Intelligence Sidebar

For each city, we show:
1. **Serviced Apartments** — N properties in our directory (live DB)
2. **Avg Monthly Rate** — from our DB price data (where available)
3. **Business Visitors** — annual international business arrivals (government/UN Tourism data)
4. **Min Stay** — typical minimum stay in this city (e.g., "1 month" for HK, "1 night" for London)
5. **Corporate Districts** — top 2–3 business districts in the city
6. **Peak Demand** — months of highest corporate demand

Sources: UN Tourism, government statistics offices, GSAIR 2025
